We deliver across Nigeria from our base at Aco Estate, Abuja.

Delivery fees

The delivery fee depends on your location and the logistics option you choose. We tell you the exact fee on WhatsApp before you pay, and it is added to your order total. Product prices on the website do not include delivery.

How delivery works

  1. You place your order and we confirm availability.
  2. We quote your delivery fee based on your address and your choice of logistics.
  3. You pay the item total plus the delivery fee [or pay on delivery where available].
  4. We pack your order and hand it to the logistics partner. We send you the rider or tracking details.
  5. You receive your order, open it and check it. If anything is wrong, follow the returns policy within 24 hours.

Good to know

  • Please give a complete address and a phone number that is reachable on the day of delivery.
  • If a delivery fails because the address is wrong or nobody answers, you may pay again for re-delivery. [Confirm.]
  • Delivery times are estimates. Delays outside our control, such as traffic, weather or courier issues, can happen.
